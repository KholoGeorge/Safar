WHEN AM I DONE?
The Arguelles test
There is no number. Two subjective signals tell you when a text has given you everything it has.
1 · The plateau

Shadow the text until you stop noticing change. Early passes sharpen a consonant, fix a vowel length, land a liaison. Later passes feel identical. That stillness is the signal, not a failure.

2 · The shower test

Can you run the whole dialogue in your head — in order, without looking — while doing something else? Walking, washing dishes, standing in the shower. If you blank at the third line, you're not there yet. One more day closes the gap.

The rhythm
Day 1–3Every pass changes something. Keep going.
Day 4–6Change slows. One pass feels like the last. Keep going.
Day 7No change. Take the shower test. If you pass it, move on.
The caveat

Boredom is not a plateau. If you've stopped improving and you dread opening Study mode again, the text is done. Grinding a dialogue you're sick of teaches you nothing. Move on.

That's the bar. Not a number. A feeling of stillness, confirmed by a shower.
